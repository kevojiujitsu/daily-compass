How can we help?

For help with Daily Compass, email kwjiujitsu@gmail.com.

Include your device type, app version, and a short description of what happened. Please do not email passwords, verification codes, private journal entries, or health records.

Your account and data

You can delete your account and saved entries from the app using “Delete my profile and all entries.” If you cannot access the app, use the account deletion page.

See the Privacy Policy for how your information is handled.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel a subscription through the store where you purchased it. Deleting your Daily Compass account does not cancel a store subscription.

Google Play subscription help · Apple subscription help

Your community

Use the report and block controls in Community to flag content or stop interactions. You can also contact support about a community concern.
