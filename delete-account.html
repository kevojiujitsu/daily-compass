Delete your Daily Compass account

You can delete your account and associated server data from the app using Settings → Delete my profile and all entries. If you no longer have access to the app, request deletion by email:

Request account deletion

Send the request from the email address associated with your account, or include that sign-in email address so we can identify the account. Never include your password. We may reply to confirm the request before deleting the account.

What will be deleted

When your request is processed, we delete the Daily Compass account and its server-stored profile, friend connections, posts, comments, reactions, and other account-associated community content. Your locally stored journal, mood, tasks, health notes, and habits are on your device; delete the app or clear its data to remove those local copies. Reports associated with other members may be retained when needed to review safety concerns, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Google Play subscriptions are billed and managed by Google Play. Deleting your Daily Compass account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
