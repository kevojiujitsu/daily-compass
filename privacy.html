Privacy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Audience: Daily Compass is intended for users age 18 and older. This policy is available at the public HTTPS address linked from the app and its store listings.

Daily Compass saves journal, mood, task, work, health, and habit entries on your device. The local app storage also contains your name, age, email address, and sign-in session token. The Daily Compass server stores your account name, age, email address, a salted password hash, hashed sign-in session tokens, and monthly AI usage counts. The server does not store journal text or AI prompts and plans. The password is used to sign in and does not encrypt entries saved on your device. No analytics are currently used.

When you request an AI journal reflection or a weekly or monthly journal summary, the app sends the selected-day entries and any included journal history, or the journal and mood-check-in entries in the selected summary period, to the app server. The server forwards them to OpenAI. When you request task organization, the app sends your open tasks and their due dates and priorities. When you request a workout plan, meal plan, or recipes, it sends the preferences and details you entered for that request, including any allergy exclusions. Apple Health and Health Connect readings are not included. Store builds use HTTPS. The app asks for permission before each AI request. Your email, name, age, and password are not included in AI requests. The app server does not save request text and requests non-stored responses from OpenAI; successful requests count toward your monthly AI allowance. OpenAI processes API data under its applicable terms and retention policies. See OpenAI’s data privacy information. If online AI is unavailable, the app shows simple guidance generated locally.

You can delete individual entries or use “Delete my profile and all entries” in the app to remove your server-side account and the local profile and entries on this device. If you no longer have access to the app, request account deletion at daily-compass-api.onrender.com/delete-account.html. Requests are verified using the account email; never send your password. Account deletion removes server-stored account and community content. Reports associated with other members may be retained as needed to investigate safety issues. Deleting a Daily Compass account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; cancel it separately in your store subscription settings.

Google Play subscriptions

If you buy or restore Compass Plus in the Android app, Google Play processes the payment and provides the app with a purchase token. The app sends that token over HTTPS to the Daily Compass server, which sends it to Google Play to verify the subscription and its status. The server does not keep the raw purchase token; it stores a SHA-256 hash of the purchase token (and any linked prior token), the product and base-plan identifiers, and the subscription expiration time to associate and manage your Compass Plus access and prevent the same subscription from being linked to multiple Daily Compass accounts. Google Play handles payment details under its own privacy terms. The app does not receive or store your payment card number.

App Store subscriptions

If you buy or restore Compass Plus on iPhone or iPad, Apple processes your payment. The app sends Apple’s signed transaction to the Daily Compass server for signature and account verification. The server stores the product, transaction and original transaction identifiers, expiry and purchase dates, and refund/revocation status associated with your Daily Compass account. Apple sends signed renewal and refund notifications to update this access. Apple handles payment details; Daily Compass does not receive your payment card number. TestFlight purchases use Apple’s sandbox and are restricted to approved test accounts by the service. Manage or cancel an Apple subscription in Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Phone notifications and reminders

Daily check-in, task/goal reminders, and encouragement are optional. Reminder times and preferences are saved on this device, and reminders are scheduled locally. Notification previews exclude journal text, task names, and health details. If you enable app announcements, the app registers this phone with Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and sends its device registration token to the Daily Compass service, where it is associated with your account to deliver announcements. Google processes the device registration and message delivery. Turn announcements off in Notifications to remove this device registration from the service; deleting your account removes its registrations. You can also block notifications in your device settings. iPhone and iPad currently support local reminders; app announcements are available only on Android. The service retains a limited history of announcements and delivery counts for the app owner. Firebase Analytics collection is disabled.

Apple Health and Health Connect

If you connect Apple Health on iPhone or iPad, or Health Connect on Android, Daily Compass requests read access only for steps, calories burned, and heart rate. The app reads those records on your device to show the health dashboard. It does not send Apple Health or Health Connect records to the Daily Compass server or to OpenAI. You can revoke access in the Apple Health app’s sharing settings or Android Health Connect settings. Availability depends on your device, operating system, permissions, and stored readings. Food calorie estimates and habits you enter manually are saved in this device’s local app storage.

Community posts, profiles, and safety

Before you share a post, comment, or community profile, the app asks permission to send that shared text, your display name, and any attached photo through the Daily Compass server to OpenAI for a safety check. Only this submitted community content is checked; private journal entries and device health readings are not included. Content that is flagged, or cannot be checked, is not published. These checks do not use your monthly AI allowance. You can cancel without sharing. Contact support to appeal a blocked submission.

If you post in Community, your text and any photo you attach are uploaded to the Daily Compass service and stored with your account on its persistent server disk. Your posts are visible to your accepted Daily Compass friends. If you choose to create a community profile, your profile photo, selected hobby icons, Spotify track link, and any optional age, hometown, quote, or relationship status you provide are also stored by the service and shown to accepted friends. Leave optional fields blank to keep them off your profile. You can edit or remove these profile details in Community. Friend requests, friend connections, comments, and reactions are stored by the service. Reports include the reported post or account, the reporting account, the reason, and the report time. Blocking removes the connection and prevents each account from seeing the other’s community posts. Reports may be reviewed by the service moderator. Posts reported by three distinct members are automatically hidden pending review. Do not share sensitive personal or medical information in community posts.

You can permanently delete your account from the app. Account deletion removes the account and its social content from the service, along with local profile and entries on this device. Reports associated with other members may be retained as needed to investigate safety issues.
