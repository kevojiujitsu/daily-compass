Privacy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Audience: Daily Compass is intended for users age 18 and older. This policy still needs final review and publication at a public HTTPS URL before store submission.

Daily Compass saves journal, mood, task, work, health, and habit entries on your device. The local app storage also contains your name, age, email address, and sign-in session token. The Daily Compass server stores your account name, age, email address, a salted password hash, hashed sign-in session tokens, and monthly AI usage counts. The server does not store journal text or AI prompts and plans. The password is used to sign in and does not encrypt entries saved on your device. No analytics are currently used.

When you request an AI journal reflection or a weekly or monthly journal summary, the app sends the selected-day entries and any included journal history, or the journal and mood-check-in entries in the selected summary period, to the app server. The server forwards them to OpenAI. When you request task organization, the app sends your open tasks and their due dates and priorities. When you request a workout plan, meal plan, or recipes, it sends the preferences and details you entered for that request, including any allergy exclusions. Health Connect readings are not included. Store builds should use HTTPS. Your email, name, age, and password are not included in AI requests. The app server does not save request text and requests non-stored responses from OpenAI; successful requests count toward your monthly AI allowance. OpenAI may process or retain API data under the account's settings and applicable policies; review the provider's current terms before release. If online AI is unavailable, the app shows simple guidance generated locally.

You can delete individual entries or use “Delete my profile and all entries” in the app to remove your server-side account and the local profile and entries on this device. There is no password reset, account recovery, or journal-entry backup in this prototype.

Optional relaxing music is generated in the app on your device. Music selection and playback are not sent to the app server.

Health Connect

If you connect Health Connect, Daily Compass requests read access only for steps, total calories burned, and heart rate. The app reads those records on your device to show the health dashboard. It does not send Health Connect records to the Daily Compass server or to OpenAI. You can revoke access in Android Health Connect settings. Food calorie estimates and habits you enter manually are saved in this device’s local app storage.

Community posts, profiles, and safety

If you post in Community, your text and any photo you attach are uploaded to the Daily Compass service and stored with your account on its persistent server disk. Your posts are visible to your accepted Daily Compass friends. If you choose to create a community profile, your profile photo, selected hobby icons, and Spotify track link are also stored by the service and shown to accepted friends. You can edit or remove these profile details in Community. Friend requests, friend connections, comments, and reactions are stored by the service. Reports include the reported post or account, the reporting account, the reason, and the report time. Blocking removes the connection and prevents each account from seeing the other’s community posts. Reports may be reviewed by the service moderator. Posts reported by three distinct members are automatically hidden pending review. Do not share sensitive personal or medical information in community posts.

You can permanently delete your account from the app. Account deletion removes the account and its social content from the service, along with local profile and entries on this device. Reports associated with other members may be retained as needed to investigate safety issues.
