Privacy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Audience: Daily Compass is intended for users age 18 and older. This policy still needs final review and publication at a public HTTPS URL before store submission.

Daily Compass saves journal, mood, task, work, health, and habit entries on your device. The local app storage also contains your name, age, email address, and sign-in session token. The Daily Compass server stores your account name, age, email address, a salted password hash, hashed sign-in session tokens, and monthly AI reflection count. The server does not store journal entry text. The password is used to sign in and does not encrypt entries saved on your device. No analytics are currently used.

When you tap “Reflect on this day + past 30 days,” Daily Compass sends entries for the selected day plus up to 60 journal entries from the previous 30 days (including their dates and optional moods) to the app server. The server forwards them to OpenAI to generate a detailed reflection and advice. Store builds should use HTTPS. Local development previews may use HTTP and should use test entries only. Your email, name, age, and password are not included in the AI request. The app server does not save entry text, requests a non-stored response from OpenAI, and records successful reflections against your monthly account allowance. OpenAI may process or retain API data under the account's settings and applicable policies; review the provider's current terms before release. If online AI is unavailable, the app shows simple guidance generated locally.

You can delete individual entries or use “Delete my profile and all entries” in the app to remove your server-side account and the local profile and entries on this device. There is no password reset, account recovery, or journal-entry backup in this prototype.

Optional relaxing music is generated in the app on your device. Music selection and playback are not sent to the app server.
