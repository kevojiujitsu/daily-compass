Privacy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Audience: Daily Compass is intended for users age 18 and older. This policy describes the current app and is available at the public HTTPS address linked from the app and its Google Play listing.

Request account deletion

You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete my profile and all entries. If you no longer have access to the app, email us to request deletion:

Request deletion of my Daily Compass account

Send the request from the email address on the account, or include that sign-in email so we can identify it. Never send your password. Account deletion removes the server account, profile, posts, comments, reactions, and friend connections. Reports needed for safety review may be retained. Journal, mood, task, health, and habit entries stored locally on your device are not removed from that device by an email request; clear the app data or uninstall the app to remove those local copies. Deleting your Daily Compass account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately under Google Play Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Information stored

Daily Compass saves journal, mood, task, work, health, and habit entries on your device. Local app storage also contains your name, age, email address, and sign-in session token. The Daily Compass server stores your account name, age, email address, a salted password hash, hashed sign-in session tokens, and monthly AI usage counts. The server does not store journal text or AI prompts and plans. The password is used to sign in and does not encrypt entries saved on your device. No analytics are currently used.

AI requests

When you request an AI journal reflection or a weekly or monthly journal summary, the app sends the selected-day entries and any included journal history, or the journal and mood-check-in entries in the selected summary period, to the app server. The server forwards them to OpenAI. For task organization, the app sends your open tasks and their due dates and priorities. For workout plans, meal plans, or recipes, it sends the preferences and details entered for that request, including any allergy exclusions. Health Connect readings are not included. Requests use HTTPS. Your email, name, age, and password are not included in AI requests. The app server does not save request text and requests non-stored responses from OpenAI; successful requests count toward your monthly AI allowance. OpenAI may process or retain API data under the account's settings and applicable policies. If online AI is unavailable, the app shows simple guidance generated locally.

Google Play subscriptions

If you buy or restore Compass Plus in the Android app, Google Play processes the payment and provides the app with a purchase token. The app sends that token over HTTPS to the Daily Compass server, which sends it to Google Play to verify the subscription and its status. The server does not keep the raw purchase token; it stores a SHA-256 hash of the purchase token and any linked prior token, the product and base-plan identifiers, and subscription expiration time to manage Compass Plus access and prevent the same subscription from being linked to multiple Daily Compass accounts. Google Play handles payment details under its own privacy terms. The app does not receive or store your payment card number.

Optional music

Relaxing music is generated in the app on your device. Music selection and playback are not sent to the app server.

Health Connect

If you connect Health Connect, Daily Compass requests read access only for steps, total calories burned, and heart rate. The app reads those records on your device to show the health dashboard. It does not send Health Connect records to the Daily Compass server or to OpenAI. You can revoke access in Android Health Connect settings. Food calorie estimates and habits you enter manually are saved in this device’s local app storage.

Community posts, profiles, and safety

If you post in Community, your text and any attached photo are uploaded to the Daily Compass service and stored with your account. Posts are visible to accepted Daily Compass friends. Your profile photo, hobby icons, Spotify track link, and optional age, hometown, quote, or relationship status are stored by the service and shown to accepted friends. Friend requests, connections, comments, reactions, and reports are stored by the service. Blocking removes the connection and prevents each account from seeing the other’s community posts. Reports may be reviewed by the service moderator. Posts reported by three distinct members are automatically hidden pending review. Do not share sensitive personal or medical information in community posts.
